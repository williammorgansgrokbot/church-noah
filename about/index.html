The idea

Their crayons. Their animals. Their story.

Kids color printed Noah pages — a lion, elephant, giraffe, sheep, duck, Noah himself, the ark, a cloud, a rainbow, a rock and a puddle. The teacher snaps one phone photo, and the app automatically cuts out each drawing and places the children's own art inside the game.

🦁 Lion🐘 Elephant🦒 Giraffe🐑 Sheep🦆 Duck🧔 Noah🚢 Ark☁️ Cloud🌈 Rainbow🪨 Rock💧 Puddle
  • Ownership — “That's MY elephant!” Children see their work honored inside the story.
  • Creativity — every class makes a one-of-a-kind version of the game.
  • Bible story retention — coloring, then playing through the story, reinforces it twice in one morning.

The game

Five gentle parts of one great story

Designed for little hands: big targets, kind sounds, and no way to lose. Every child finishes the story and reaches the rainbow.

Part 1

Build the Ark

Children help Noah put the ark together piece by piece — obeying God even before the rain.

Part 2

Two by Two

The class's own colored animals march into the ark in pairs.

Part 3

Ride the Flood

Steer the ark safely through the waters — God keeps Noah's family safe.

Part 4

The Dove's Flight

Guide the dove to find dry land and bring back the olive leaf.

Part 5

The Rainbow Finale

A celebration of God's promise, with the verse on screen to read together.

“I have put my rainbow in the clouds. It will be the sign of the covenant between me and the earth.”
Genesis 9:13 (NIrV)

Safety & privacy

Protecting our children comes first

🖍️ Only drawings

Only the kids' crayon drawings are published. No photos of children and no names — ever.

🔐 A locked-down key

The publishing key is stored in Windows Credential Manager, limited to this one repository, and can be revoked at any time.

🚫 No tracking

No accounts, no ads, and no data collection. The game simply runs in the browser.
